# Tone of voice

UI copy is written in **Catalan, Spanish and English** (`ca` / `es` / `en`). The same rules
apply in every language.

## Rules

1. **Calm, not cheerful.** People may be looking at messages from someone who died. No
   exclamation marks, emojis or celebrations around memories.
2. **Plain words.** Say *photos*, *messages*, *copy*, *file* — never *blobs*, *OPFS*,
   *fingerprints* or *batches*.
3. **Reassure about data.** Every destructive or risky action says what will happen and what
   is kept.
4. **Name people, not records.** "Mama's messages", not "Profile 3 entries".
5. **Short and direct.** One idea per sentence; buttons are verbs.

## Before / after

| Before | After |
| ------ | ----- |
| "Import batch #12 committed! 🎉" | "4,210 new messages added to Mama." |
| "Deduplication skipped 8,240 records." | "8,240 messages were already in the archive and were not copied again." |
| "Delete profile?" | "Remove Papa from the archive? His messages and photos stay in your last backup." |
| "OPFS quota exceeded." | "There is not enough space on this device. Try importing without videos." |
